About Giveaway Shuffle

Giveaway Shuffle is a Discord giveaway bot focused on fairness, suspenseful live drawings, advanced rerolls, and full giveaway control.

Perfect for livestreams, community events, ticket raffles, and real-world giveaways.

Features

Manual & Bulk Entries

Add single contestants or bulk entries directly through /sentry.

Weighted Drawings

Every entry becomes a ticket in a fully shuffled drawing pool.

Live Elimination Reveals

Top finalists are eliminated live one-by-one for suspenseful winner reveals.

Multi-Place Winners

Supports 1st, 2nd, 3rd place and more with individual placement drawings.

Advanced Rerolls

Replace individual winners while preserving original placements.

Scheduled Closing

Automatically stop entries at your chosen time.

Delayed Drawings

Close entries now and reveal winners later.

Giveaway Tracking

Track active giveaways, past drawings, and winner history.

Permission Protection

Improved private channel support and giveaway management protection.

Fair Drawing System

Giveaway Shuffle uses a multi-stage weighted drawing system designed to balance fairness, suspense, and placement-based winner selection.

  • Every entry becomes its own ticket.
  • More entries improve the odds of reaching the finalist pool.
  • The finalist pool contains unique contestants only.
  • Finalists are eliminated live one-by-one.
  • Multi-place giveaways draw each placement separately.
  • Rerolls preserve original winner placements.

Commands

/screate
Create a new giveaway.
/sdesc
Edit giveaway description.
/sentry
Add single or bulk entries.
/sremove
Remove a contestant and all entries.
/stime
Adjust close and drawing times.
/sclose
Close giveaway entries.
/sdraw
Run drawing immediately.
/sreroll
Reroll winners while preserving placements.
/slist
View giveaways, drawings, and winner history.
/sdelete
Delete a giveaway.
/shelp
Show command help.

Required Permissions

For Giveaway Shuffle to work properly in a channel, the bot (or its role) should have:

  • View Channel
  • Send Messages
  • Read Message History
  • Embed Links

For private channels, Giveaway Shuffle must also be added under the channel's Roles/Members permissions.
